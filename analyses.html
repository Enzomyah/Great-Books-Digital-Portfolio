Section 02 - Literary Analyses

The Literary
Archive.

Seven Works / Seven Interpretations

Each analysis in this archive looks at a great story through a specific literary lens - a helpful tool or angle that changes how we read and understand a text. Together, these seven analyses show how looking at stories from different sides helps us better understand human life.

01

The Odyssey

Author: Homer

Archetypal / Mythological Lens

Looks at Odysseus's long voyage home through the classic hero's journey pattern, studying his dangerous tests, the help he gets from Athena, and how he grows before reclaiming his home.

Read
02

The Ramayana

Author: Traditionally attributed to Valmiki

Feminist Lens

Examines Sita's agency, moral resilience, and emotional endurance within patriarchal expectations, analyzing gender dynamics in ancient Indian epic tradition.

Read
03

The Mahabharata

Author: Traditionally attributed to Vyasa

Psychoanalytic Lens

Explores how jealousy, pride, guilt, and deep family grudges drive the tragic war between the Pandavas and Kauravas.

Read
04

The Panchatantra

Author: Traditionally attributed to Vishnu Sharma

Formalist / New Critical Lens

Focuses on story structure, talking animal characters, and stories-within-stories, analyzing how the storytelling style delivers practical lessons for daily life.

Read
05

The Song of Roland

Author: Anonymous

Historical / Contextual Lens

Analyzes medieval European feudal loyalty, knightly codes of honor, and religious warfare during the era of Charlemagne and the Crusades.

Read
06

The Tale of Genji

Author: Murasaki Shikibu

Biographical Lens

Examines how Murasaki Shikibu's own life, literary education, and experiences as an observant Heian court lady-in-waiting shaped her psychological narrative.

Read
07

The Book of Job

Author: Authorship uncertain

Ecocritical Lens

Examines humanity's place within the non-human cosmos, wild creatures, and meteorological wonders in the Voice Out of the Whirlwind (Job 38–41).
