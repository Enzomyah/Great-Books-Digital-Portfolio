Literary References

01

The Odyssey - Homer

Homer. (1996). The Odyssey (R. Fagles, Trans.). Penguin Books. (Original work composed c. 8th century BCE).
Secondary translation: Homer. (2017). The Odyssey (E. Wilson, Trans.). W. W. Norton & Company.
Critical reference: Fowler, R. (Ed.). (2004). The Cambridge Companion to Homer. Cambridge University Press.
SDG Connections: United Nations (SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities, SDG 16: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions).

02

The Ramayana - Valmiki

Valmiki. (1990). The Ramayana of Valmiki: An Epic of Ancient India (R. P. Goldman, Trans. & Ed.). Princeton University Press.
Narayan, R. K. (2006). The Ramayana: A Shortened Modern Prose Version of the Indian Epic. Penguin Classics.
Class References: Oxford Bibliographies (Rāmāyaṇa in the Hindu Tradition); UNESCO Courier (Two Epics of Asia); Shared Histories Asia (How Did Cultures Mix); The Dr. Binocs Show (Story of Ramayana); United Nations (SDG 5, SDG 16).

03

The Mahabharata - Vyasa

Vyasa. (1965). The Mahabharata: An English Version Based on Selected Verses (C. V. Narasimhan, Trans.). Columbia University Press. (Original work composed c. 4th century BCE–4th century CE).
Scholarly translation: van Buitenen, J. A. B. (Trans. & Ed.). (1973–1978). The Mahabharata (Vols. 1–3). University of Chicago Press.
Supplementary: Das, G. (2009). The Difficulty of Being Good: On the Subtle Art of Dharma. Oxford University Press.
Supplementary: Matilal, B. K. (Ed.). (1989). Moral Dilemmas in the Mahabharata. Indian Institute of Advanced Study / Motilal Banarsidass.

04

The Panchatantra - Vishnu Sharma

Vishnu Sharma. (1993). The Panchatantra (C. Rajan, Trans.). Penguin Classics. (Original work compiled c. 3rd century BCE).
Translations: Ryder, A. W. (Trans.). (1925). The Panchatantra. University of Chicago Press.
Comparative edition: Olivelle, P. (Trans.). (1997). Pancatantra: The Book of India's Folk Wisdom. Oxford University Press.
SDG Connection: United Nations (SDG 16: Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions).

05

The Song of Roland - Anonymous

Anonymous. (1990). The Song of Roland (G. S. Burgess, Trans.). Penguin Classics. (Original work c. 1100).
Class References: Encyclopaedia Britannica (La chanson de Roland, Roncesvalles); Simple English Wikipedia (Song of Roland); LitCharts (Song of Roland); SparkNotes (Characters); GradeSaver (Character List); Wikipedia (Battle of Roncevaux Pass, Ibalong Epic); Britannica Kids (Roland); Philippine Cultural Education Online (Handiong); Cultural Center of the Philippines (Our Lady of Peñafrancia); United Nations (SDG 16).

06

The Tale of Genji - Murasaki Shikibu

Murasaki Shikibu. (2001). The Tale of Genji (R. Tyler, Trans.). Viking / Penguin Books. (Original work composed c. 1000–1012 CE).
Class References: Columbia University Asia for Educators (The Tale of Genji); Tale of Genji Website (Summary); Nippon.com (Heart of Japanese Culture); United Nations (SDG 5, SDG 10).

07

The Book of Job - Hebrew Scripture

Mitchell, S. (Trans.). (1987). The Book of Job. Harper Perennial. (Original work composed c. 7th–4th century BCE).
Literary translation: Alter, R. (Trans.). (2010). The Wisdom Books: Job, Proverbs, and Ecclesiastes: A Translation with Commentary. W. W. Norton & Company.
Critical commentary: Newsom, C. A. (2003). The Book of Job: A Contest of Moral Imaginations. Oxford University Press.

External Research & Critical Theory

R1

Archetypal / Mythological Lens - Campbell, J. (1949)

Campbell, J. (2004). The Hero with a Thousand Faces (Commemorative ed.). Princeton University Press. (Original work published 1949). Foundations of the Monomyth and cross-cultural archetypes applied to ancient epic journeys.
Applied in Analysis 01: The Odyssey

R2

Feminist Criticism (Epic & Heroic Traditions) - Richman, P. (1991) & Sutherland, S. J. (1989)

Richman, P. (Ed.). (1991). Many Ramayanas: The Diversity of a Narrative Tradition in South Asia. University of California Press. Feminist analyses of Sita's agency, voice, moral trials, and patriarchal constraints.
Applied in Analysis 02: The Ramayana

R3

Psychoanalytic Criticism - Freud, S. (1923) & Jung, C. G. (1959)

Freud, S. (1960). The Ego and the Id (J. Riviere, Trans.; J. Strachey, Ed.). W. W. Norton & Company. (Original work published 1923). Structural theory of Id, Ego, and Superego drives.
Applied in Analysis 03: The Mahabharata

R4

Formalist / New Criticism - Brooks, C. & Warren, R. P.

Brooks, C., & Warren, R. P. (1979). Understanding Fiction (3rd ed.). Prentice-Hall. Principles of frame-tale architecture, organic unity, tension, and close textual mechanics.
Applied in Analysis 04: The Panchatantra

R5

Historical / Contextual Criticism (Medieval Chivalric Literature) - Burgess, G. (1990) & Bloch, R. H. (1977)

Bloch, R. H. (1977). Medieval French Literature and Law. University of California Press. Feudal jurisprudence, crusader ideology, and chivalric honor codes in Old French heroic chansons de geste.
Applied in Analysis 05: The Song of Roland

R6

Biographical Criticism & Heian Court Literature - Bowring, R. (1996)

Bowring, R. (Trans. & Ed.). (1996). The Diary of Lady Murasaki. Penguin Classics. Biographical documentation, court observations, and personal reflections of Murasaki Shikibu informing The Tale of Genji.
Applied in Analysis 06: The Tale of Genji

R7

Ecocriticism & Environmental Hermeneutics - Habel, N. C. (2001) & Glotfelty, C. (1996)

Habel, N. C. (Ed.). (2001). The Earth Bible: The Earth Story in Wisdom Traditions. Sheffield Academic Press. Ecocritical readings of Job 38–41, meteorological creation, and wild nature decentering human autonomy.
Applied in Analysis 07: The Book of Job
