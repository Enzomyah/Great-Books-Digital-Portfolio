A - A Little About Me

Hello! I'm Earl Lawrence C. Cipres, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student. I enjoy learning new things, exploring technology, and discovering different ways to express ideas. As a student, I believe that learning does not only happen inside the classroom but also through the stories we read and the experiences we gain.

Through Great Books, I have the opportunity to explore different literary works, understand different perspectives, and learn more about people, society, and life.

B - My Interests & Literary Experiences

My interests include photography, music, fitness, and reading e-books. I enjoy creative activities because they allow me to explore ideas and express myself in different ways.

Reading literary works also gives me an opportunity to discover different characters, experiences, and viewpoints. Even when a story comes from a different time or culture, its themes can still connect to real-life situations. Analyzing these works helps me look beyond the story and think about the deeper meaning behind it.

C - My Expectations in Great Books

Before taking Great Books, I expected to learn about important literary masterpieces and understand the stories behind them. Throughout the course, I wanted to improve my ability to analyze literature, recognize different themes, and understand how literary works reflect human experiences.

I also hoped to develop my critical thinking skills and become more open to different perspectives. Through this subject, I have learned that literature is not only about reading stories but also about understanding the lessons, ideas, and experiences they share with readers.
